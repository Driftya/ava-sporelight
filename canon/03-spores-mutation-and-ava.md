## Origin and Spread

The alien organism reaches Earth through a meteor. Ava encounters its biological expression while working as a botanist in a jungle environment. Human experimentation concentrates and manipulates the organism. The laboratory breach becomes a major catalyst for worldwide spread.

Spores reshape plants, animals, and humans. The environment becomes an active carrier: forests change, animals transport contamination, and abandoned human spaces become new ecosystems.

Cold suppresses spore activity but does not make an area absolutely safe. This drives survivors toward tundra and polar regions while rescue teams continue operating in warmer contaminated zones.

## Exposure and Infection

Canonical exposure routes are:

- inhalation of a sufficiently concentrated airborne load;
- infected fluid entering a meaningful wound;
- sustained close exposure in a contaminated environment.

Gas masks, sealed clothing, filtration, quarantine, and spore sensors reduce risk. A mutant killed by ordinary means may release a fresh cloud of spores, so lethal force can turn one immediate victory into a wider contamination event.

The older development claim that every trace exposure causes mutation is rejected. Protection and dosage matter, or rescue operations would be impossible.

## Ordinary Mutation

For an ordinary human, mutation is a progressive loss of biological and neurological integrity. Common stages include illness, visible vein or tissue changes, organ and immune failure, memory or behavioral breakdown, bodily deformation, and death or feral mutation.

Exact percentages and fixed time ranges from early development notes are not canon. Progress depends on exposure, physiology, environment, and narrative circumstances.

Mutated creatures retain varying amounts of host anatomy or behavior but are driven primarily by aggression and propagation. They are biological organisms, not magical beings.

## Ava’s Exception

Ava is immune to the ordinary fatal rejection phase of mutation. Her body does not purge the spores successfully, nor does it collapse in the usual way. It integrates them and remains alive.

That survival is not immunity to change. Ava is already mutated, and the mutation remains active and progressive. Her human immune response and spore-driven regeneration exist in a volatile equilibrium, producing:

- accelerated healing;
- enhanced strength, speed, reflexes, and durability;
- amber bioluminescence in her eyes and veins;
- toxic blood and other body fluids;
- stress-driven spikes, chitin-like structures, claws, and increasingly predatory movement;
- extreme energy use, pain, exhaustion, and psychological strain.

Cryogenic suspension slowed the conflict long enough for a survivable equilibrium to form. It did not cure her.

## Transformation and Loss of Control

The transformation is both episodic and progressive.

- **Episodic:** acute fear, pain, anger, trauma, or heavy spore exposure can cause a rapid monstrous change. Ava may recover when she is grounded and the stress falls.
- **Progressive:** each stage reveals a direction her biology can continue taking. Time and repeated loss of control risk making the monstrous state stronger, longer, and eventually permanent.

The central danger is that Ava can survive long enough to become a real monster while losing the human control ordinary victims lose through degradation. Other infected people commonly deteriorate toward death or ferality; Ava’s body keeps rebuilding itself as it changes.

Gabriel can help ground her, but he is not a cure and does not possess control over her body. Ava’s learned regulation, choices, relationships, and any future treatment determine whether she can continue returning to herself.

## Toxic Biology

Ava’s fresh blood and bodily fluids are lethal to spore-mutated life forms. A neutralized mutant does not produce the same dangerous spore burst associated with ordinary killing.

Established limitations:

- Potency is strongest while the blood is fresh and biologically connected to Ava.
- Direct use often requires Ava to injure herself.
- Stored blood is scientifically valuable but is not an unlimited battlefield weapon.
- Mutated spikes and protrusions share the toxic property.
- Contact is dangerous to mutants, not automatically to uninfected humans.
- Heavy spore exposure cannot infect Ava in the ordinary sense, but it can destabilize her and accelerate transformation.

## Research and Vaccine

Hospital records provide partial research methods and blood-assay context. Jenna connects those notes to Ava’s medical data. Karrow proposes controlled extraction and study.

Ava’s consent distinguishes this work from the original experiments. She sets conditions and allows Gabriel, under Jenna’s guidance, to draw blood. Two years later, a limited vaccine derived from her blood is helping communities resist or treat the alien mutation.

The vaccine is effective but scarce. It does not instantly restore the planet, reverse every existing mutant, or eliminate environmental spores.

## Unknowns

The following remain intentionally undefined:

- the exact extraterrestrial taxonomy of the spores;
- whether the organism has collective intelligence or only adaptive biological behavior;
- the precise molecular mechanism of Ava’s toxic fluids;
- whether her progressive transformation can be permanently halted;
- what biological traits, if any, Ava’s daughter inherits;
- whether the vaccine prevents infection, arrests early mutation, or performs both functions in different doses.
