## Ava

### Identity

- Full name: Ava; surname unknown.
- Reference age at infection: 22.
- Original profession: botanist.
- Original eyes: emerald green.
- Post-mutation eyes: permanently amber, brightening when the mutation is active.
- Post-mutation hair: dark, ink-like hair with silver strands.
- Build: slim, agile, and visibly strong rather than bulky.

Ava is not an engineered person or an artificial organism. She was human before infection. Researchers selected her because she survived initial exposure unusually well, then forced the concentrated experimentation that created her current condition.

### Character center

Ava’s central conflict is ownership of herself. Other people repeatedly define her as patient, threat, weapon, cure, or monster. Her arc is not proving that she is harmless; she is genuinely dangerous. Her victory is gaining enough control, trust, and agency to decide what she does with that danger.

She is guarded, frightened, empathetic, curious, and resilient. Botany and the greenhouse reconnect her with the self she had before captivity. Fighting is something she chooses because she can protect others, not the only thing that gives her value.

### Mutation pressure

Ava survives the mutation process that kills or degrades ordinary hosts, but survival does not freeze the mutation. It continues to develop over time. Stress and trauma can force temporary leaps into a more monstrous form and weaken her control. If that progression is left unchecked, the danger is not merely an episode: she may lose her stable human identity and become a true monster.

This distinction is author-decided canon.

## Gabriel

- Full name: Gabriel; surname unknown.
- Reference age: 26.
- Former profession: firefighter.
- Current role: rescue operative, field leader, and later Ava’s trainer and partner.
- Appearance: athletic build, dark-brown hair, blue eyes; usually wears rescue equipment.

Gabriel is calm in crisis, physically capable, trained in rescue and first aid, and willing to disobey protocol when procedure abandons a living person. His strongest trait and most dangerous weakness are the same: he puts himself between danger and other people.

The loss of his parents to mutation drives his rescue work. He carries survivor’s guilt and distrusts authority when rules become an excuse not to act. His protection of Ava must not erase her agency. Their healthier progression occurs when he teaches, supports, and remains present while accepting decisions that belong to her.

## Jenna

- Full name: Dr. Jenna; surname unknown.
- Reference age: 34.
- Roles: commander/captain, scientist, and operational leader aboard Haven’s Vanguard.
- Expertise: virology, mutation biology, crisis command, navigation, and rescue planning.

Jenna begins from protocol. At the fortress and during quarantine, that makes her treatment of Ava clinical and sometimes harmful. Her arc is not a sudden personality reversal: she learns that scientific caution and recognition of personhood must coexist.

She carries responsibility for everyone aboard the carrier. Her decisions can appear cold because she weighs collective survival, but she is capable of apology and changed behavior. She becomes an important guard on the ethics of Karrow’s proposal.

Supporting canon not yet dramatized in detail: Jenna lost a younger sister to the infection, helping explain why rescue and mutation research are inseparable for her.

## Dr. Elias Karrow

- Full name: Dr. Elias Karrow.
- Reference age: 42.
- Role: biologist and researcher specializing in disease, virology, and mutation.
- Combat status: non-combatant.

Karrow joins with a later group of survivors and recognizes that Ava’s blood may enable a treatment. He is intellectually forceful and scientifically fascinated by her. His proposal echoes the language of the people who harmed Ava, whether or not his intentions are different.

Karrow is not granted automatic moral trust by his expertise. The canonical safeguards are Ava’s consent, Jenna’s oversight, Gabriel’s presence, minimal extraction, and Ava’s right to stop. His function is to make ethical science possible only after the story confronts unethical science.

## Harrow

- Full name: Harrow; surname unknown.
- Reference age: 37.
- Role: scavenger and temporary guide during the hospital mission.

Harrow survives through caution, evasiveness, improvisation, and opportunism rather than strength. He can display flashes of sympathy, but self-preservation wins when he steals part of the hospital research and abandons the group.

Harrow’s betrayal should remain human in scale. He is neither a scientific mastermind nor a mutant. He demonstrates that desperation, greed, and fear can threaten survivors as seriously as the alien ecology.

## Relationship Progression

### Ava and Gabriel

1. **Fear and rescue:** Gabriel discovers Ava but fears what she can do.
2. **Recognition:** he distinguishes her fear from deliberate violence.
3. **Trust under pressure:** each saves the other; Gabriel refuses to leave her at the fortress.
4. **Belonging:** the greenhouse and shared conversations let them know one another beyond crisis.
5. **Separation reveals attachment:** Gabriel’s absence makes Ava recognize how deeply his presence grounds her.
6. **Partnership:** Ava trains and joins missions rather than remaining someone Gabriel only protects.
7. **Confession:** both explicitly choose love after the factory settlement.
8. **Intimacy:** their first sexual relationship follows the confession and remains rooted in reassurance and consent.
9. **Shared danger:** the hospital mission tests them as an established couple.
10. **Shared future:** Ava’s research decision remains hers; their later family is chosen, not imposed.

### Ava and Jenna

The line is **subject of fear → uneasy passenger → useful crewmate → person seen clearly → trusted collaborator**. Jenna’s apology matters because it names her earlier failure without demanding Ava immediately erase its harm.

### Ava and the crew

Acceptance is gradual and uneven. The greenhouse changes opinion because it reveals knowledge, patience, and contribution, but it does not make the danger disappear. Some crew members may continue to fear Ava even while accepting that she belongs.
