## Purpose

This ledger preserves decisions that a future editor or agent must understand before changing continuity. It records the result and rationale without depending on external files or earlier drafts.

## Author-Decided Canon

1. **Haven’s Vanguard is not a spacecraft.** It is a terrestrial/atmospheric Titan-class hover carrier. Star and void language in the manuscript is night imagery.
2. **Ava is immune to the ordinary fatal mutation process, not to transformation.** Her body survives and integrates the mutation, but she continues becoming more monstrous over time and can lose control. Ordinary mutants degrade; Ava’s anomaly lets her survive the progression, creating the risk of a permanent monster state.
3. **`canon/` constrains `manuscript/`.** Manuscript additions may elaborate canon but cannot contradict it without a deliberate author-approved canon change.
4. **The unified novel is the true light novel edition.** Earlier divisions into separate stories do not govern its presentation or chapter numbering.

## Resolved Contradictions

| Topic | Conflicting interpretation | Canon resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ava’s origin | Engineered being versus naturally human botanist | Ava was born human, became infected, and was then subjected to forced experiments. |
| Ava’s immunity | Immune to mutation versus visibly transformed | She is immune to ordinary fatal rejection and degradation, but not to ongoing transformation or loss of control. |
| Mutation stability | Permanently stabilized versus episodic danger | Cryosleep created a survivable equilibrium, not a cure. Transformation remains progressive and stress-reactive. |
| The carrier | Atmospheric Titan-class versus literal space travel | Haven’s Vanguard is a hover carrier; celestial language is descriptive night imagery. |
| Fortress name | Generic fortress versus a proposed proper name | The novel establishes only “the fortress.” A proper name remains reserved. |
| Ava’s journal | Ava uses the journal before receiving it | Gabriel gives Ava the journal before she writes in it. |
| Journal attribution | Marcus versus Gabriel as giver | Gabriel gives the journal; Marcus only brings greenhouse supplies. |
| Gabriel’s past | Dialogue refers to a missing earlier exchange | The conversation now begins from Ava’s questions and occurs in the correct tense. |
| Hospital research | Recovered notes appear disconnected from later science | Jenna connects the surviving hospital notes to blood analysis that supports Karrow’s proposal. |
| Dodge and guard | Both actions had the same definition | Dodge is evasion; guard is bracing or shielding. |
| Harrow and Karrow | Similar names risk implied identity or relation | They are distinct, unrelated characters with different functions. |

## Consolidation Decisions

- The story chronology is maintained once in `01-story-and-timeline.md` rather than repeated across separate outlines.
- Character information is consolidated by character in `02-characters-and-relationships.md`.
- Ava’s immunity, progressive mutation, toxic biology, and vaccine logic are reconciled in `03-spores-mutation-and-ava.md`.
- Canonical mutant classes and unused naming ideas are separated in `03b-mutant-bestiary.md`.
- Only locations and social rules relevant to this novel are established in `04-world-locations-and-society.md`.
- Carrier specifications, rescue doctrine, and combat rules are unified in `05-ship-rescue-and-combat.md`.
- Combat techniques shown in the manuscript are distinguished from techniques that are merely available for later use.
- Editorial constraints are kept in `06-continuity-and-writing-guide.md` rather than mixed with world facts.
- Repeated introductions, greenhouse explanations, and equivalent emotional beats were removed during the chapter 15–16 integration.
- Mature language and content were retained rather than softened.

## Reserved or Unestablished Material

The following do not enter novel canon without an explicit author decision:

- sequel-only characters, events, institutions, and locations;
- separate stories about Gabriel or the next generation;
- game missions, mechanics, statistics, item tiers, and reward systems;
- named factions not used in the manuscript;
- a universal ration or power-cell economy;
- alternate creature names that have not appeared in prose;
- exact mutation fatality percentages or rigid symptom timelines;
- a proper name for the frozen fortress;
- the inheritance of Ava’s mutation by her daughter;
- an exact mechanism for the vaccine beyond what the manuscript establishes.

## Recording Future Decisions

Add a new entry when the author makes a durable continuity decision. Each entry should contain:

- the date;
- the status: author-decided, established, supporting canon, unknown, or reserved;
- the decision in one unambiguous sentence;
- the canon and manuscript files affected;
- any earlier rule it replaces.

Do not record temporary drafting suggestions or infer author intent from unused material.
