# Ava: Sporelight Canon

This directory is the authoritative reference for the unified *Ava: Sporelight* light novel. It provides a self-contained canon that can be followed from origin to epilogue.

## Authority Order

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. A current, explicit author decision overrides earlier project material.
2. The story documents in `canon/` constrain the manuscript’s rules, identities, chronology, and interpretations.
3. The current chapter files in `manuscript/` establish the prose and events already on the page, provided they do not conflict with canon.
4. Documents in `canon/visual/` govern how canonical material is depicted, but they do not override story canon or chapter events.
5. Files in `concepts/` support visual consistency; they are development references, not independent story canon or publishable manuscript assets.
6. External drafts, game material, sequel plans, prompts, and image notes do not change novel canon by implication.

If the manuscript and canon conflict, canon governs and the manuscript should be flagged for correction. Do not silently change canon to excuse a draft contradiction. A deliberate author decision that changes continuity must update canon first, or in the same change, before the manuscript adopts it.

## Status Language

- **Established:** directly shown or stated in the unified manuscript.
- **Author-decided:** explicitly clarified by the author, even if the manuscript still needs clearer wording.
- **Supporting canon:** promoted from development notes because it fits the manuscript without contradiction.
- **Unknown:** intentionally undecided or not established.
- **Reserved:** development material that is not canon for this novel.

## One-Sentence Canon

After alien spores and human experimentation turn botanist Ava into a progressively mutating survivor, she wakes two centuries into a ruined world and must reclaim her body, her place among Gabriel’s crew, and her right to choose whether the same biology threatening her humanity can help humanity begin again.

## Core Narrative Line

**origin → rescue → belonging → fighting together → love → betrayal and discovery → chosen sacrifice → a future together**
