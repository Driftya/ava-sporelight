# Ava: Sporelight Canon

This directory is the authoritative reference for the unified *Ava: Sporelight* light novel.

## Authority Order

1. A current, explicit author decision overrides earlier project material.
2. The story documents in `canon/public/` constrain the manuscript’s rules, identities, chronology, and interpretations.
3. The current chapter files in `manuscript/` establish the prose and events already on the page, provided they do not conflict with canon.
4. Documents in `canon/internal/visual/` govern depiction and production, but do not override story canon or chapter events.
5. Files in `concepts/` support visual consistency; they are development references, not independent story canon.
6. External drafts, game material, sequel plans, prompts, and image notes do not change novel canon by implication.

If manuscript and canon conflict, canon governs and the manuscript should be flagged for correction. A deliberate continuity change must update canon and the decision ledger before the manuscript adopts it.

## Status Language

- **Established:** directly shown or stated in the unified manuscript.
- **Author-decided:** explicitly clarified by the author.
- **Supporting canon:** promoted from development notes because it fits without contradiction.
- **Unknown:** intentionally undecided or not established.
- **Reserved:** development material that is not canon for this novel.

## One-Sentence Canon

After alien spores and human experimentation turn botanist Ava into a progressively mutating survivor, she wakes two centuries into a ruined world and must reclaim her body, her place among Gabriel’s crew, and her right to choose whether the same biology threatening her humanity can help humanity begin again.

## Core Narrative Line

**origin → rescue → belonging → fighting together → love → betrayal and discovery → chosen sacrifice → a future together**

For image production, read the target chapter, relevant public story canon, and the internal visual guideline. Select only relevant anchors from `concepts/`; written story canon and chapter events always take precedence over a conflicting visual reference.
