# Ava: Sporelight Canon

This directory is the authoritative reference for the unified *Ava: Sporelight* light novel. It replaces the scattered and sometimes contradictory notes under `temp_source/dataset` with a manuscript-first canon that can be followed from origin to epilogue.

## Authority Order

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. The current files in `manuscript/` establish what happens on the page.
2. The documents in `canon/` establish intended rules, identities, chronology, and interpretations.
3. Material in `temp_source/` is provenance or development material, not authority unless promoted here.
4. Game missions, sequel outlines, prompts, and image notes do not change novel canon by implication.

If the manuscript and canon conflict, flag the conflict. Do not silently rewrite either one. A deliberate author decision should update this canon first and the manuscript second.

## Status Language

- **Established:** directly shown or stated in the unified manuscript.
- **Author-decided:** explicitly clarified by the author, even if the manuscript still needs clearer wording.
- **Supporting canon:** promoted from development notes because it fits the manuscript without contradiction.
- **Unknown:** intentionally undecided or not established.
- **Reserved:** development material that is not canon for this novel.

## One-Sentence Canon

After alien spores and human experimentation turn botanist Ava into a progressively mutating survivor, she wakes two centuries into a ruined world and must reclaim her body, her place among Gabriel’s crew, and her right to choose whether the same biology threatening her humanity can help humanity begin again.

## Core Narrative Line

**origin → rescue → belonging → fighting together → love → betrayal and discovery → chosen sacrifice → a future together**
