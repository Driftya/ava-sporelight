## Haven’s Vanguard

**Authoritative classification:** EHC-17 *Haven’s Vanguard* is a Titan-class terrestrial and atmospheric hover carrier. It is not a spacecraft.

The manuscript frequently calls it “the ship.” References to stars beyond viewports, drifting through the night, or the surrounding void describe the night sky and the emotional isolation of travel. They do not establish orbital or interplanetary operation.

Supporting dimensions from the design notes:

- length: approximately 300 metres;
- height: approximately 80 metres;
- width: approximately 120 metres;
- empty mass: approximately 250,000 tonnes;
- propulsion: four main turbofan hover engines with lateral thrusters for control;
- structure: reinforced alloy and composite armour;
- power: compact fusion core with auxiliary solar and generator systems.

These numbers are supporting canon and may be revised if they create physical inconsistencies in a later technical pass.

## Carrier Spaces

Established or promoted spaces include:

- bridge and command stations;
- docking/vehicle bay and airlock;
- engineering bay and engine access;
- medical bay;
- mess hall and central lounge;
- crew and passenger quarters;
- observation deck/viewports;
- cargo and storage holds;
- Ava’s greenhouse, constructed in repurposed cargo space.

The carrier functions as transport, rescue base, shelter, workshop, clinic, and eventually a symbol of recovery. It can cross hostile terrain and remain airborne, but fuel, maintenance, weight, and weather must matter.

## Rescue Doctrine

The mission priority is:

1. locate and evacuate living civilians;
2. prevent contamination and unnecessary spore release;
3. maintain a viable extraction route;
4. recover medicine, food, filters, power, and critical research when it does not abandon survivors;
5. return crew and passengers to a controlled safe environment.

Teams usually operate in pairs, expanding for high-risk missions. They favor stealth, sensors, planned routes, and distraction over lethal engagement. Killing mutants is sometimes necessary, but ordinary kills can contaminate the area.

Gabriel’s conflict with Jenna after the fortress is a doctrine failure: protocol protected the carrier and survivors already aboard, but it also permitted departure without confirming whether Gabriel and Ava were alive.

## Ava in Combat

### Established methods

- Direct biological contact or fresh blood neutralizes mutants.
- She can cut herself to bring toxic blood into a fight, but doing so carries pain and cumulative cost.
- Her transformed spikes can pierce and neutralize mutant tissue.
- Enhanced strength lets her strike, throw, brace, and manipulate heavy debris.
- Enhanced speed and reflexes support evasion.
- Training teaches stance, footwork, weapon handling, threat anticipation, restraint, and emotional regulation.
- She prefers controlled action over surrendering to the mutated form.

### Supporting but not yet dramatized as standard practice

- coating a blade with fresh blood;
- using debris to create barriers or indirect attacks;
- luring mutants into prepared traps;
- creating very short-lived deterrent lines with fresh blood.

These tactics may be used later, but they must respect the limited potency and bodily cost of Ava’s blood.

## Gabriel in Combat

Gabriel is a rescuer first. His combat style protects civilians, creates movement windows, and avoids spore release.

Established methods include firearms used for distraction or controlled defense, close rescue under pressure, tactical commands, first aid, route planning, and physically shielding others. Supporting equipment may include tranquilizer darts, flash devices, smoke, snares, and light exosuit assistance.

Gabriel remains fully vulnerable to infection. Mask integrity, skin protection, replacement filters, and decontamination are mandatory. His equipment favors mobility and casualty extraction over heavy armour.

## Resolved Combat Duplicates

The old combat notes gave “Dodge” and “Guard” the same description. Canon separates them:

- **Dodge:** use speed, anticipation, and footwork to leave the attack path.
- **Guard:** brace with enhanced strength or use an object as a shield when escape is impossible or someone else needs cover.

Speculative combat moves in old notes are options, not abilities Ava has already mastered. Game move identifiers such as `BioCoatedBlade`, `SpikeMutation`, and `TrapLure` are implementation labels, not prose terminology.
