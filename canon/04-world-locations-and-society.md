## The Fallen World

Two centuries of mutation have produced a planet where human ruins and alien ecology overlap. Cities are overgrown, buried, frozen, or hollowed into scavenging grounds. Mutated vegetation can be beautiful as well as dangerous; bioluminescence is a recurring visual language for the new environment.

Humanity survives through mobile rescue crews, fortified cold-region settlements, isolated outposts, and small improvised communities. The world is damaged, not empty. Its central social tension is whether survival produces cooperation, authoritarian control, exploitation, or betrayal.

## Established Locations

### Jungle research region

Ava’s original botanical work and the underground laboratory are located in a humid jungle region. The lab becomes a tomb after containment fails. Its exact country and coordinates are unknown.

### Ruined town and discovery site

Gabriel discovers Ava during a civilian evacuation in a settlement consumed by alien growth. Her cryopod remains functional inside the ruins. This area is cold enough to require protective gear but still supports active mutants.

### Fortress in the frozen region

The fortress is a large organized refuge governed by strict security and quarantine practices. It is vulnerable despite the cold. The outbreak and structural collapse demonstrate that fortification without trust or adaptable judgment is not safety.

The development name “Icehold Citadel” is reserved, not established in the manuscript.

### Haven’s Vanguard

The rescue carrier is the novel’s mobile sanctuary. See [Haven’s Vanguard, Rescue Operations, and Combat](05-ship-rescue-and-combat.md).

### Abandoned outposts and convenience-store ruins

These smaller missions sustain the carrier and provide Ava’s first controlled field experience. Exact names and coordinates are unknown.

### Factory settlement

Survivors have fortified an old factory with scrap metal and barbed wire. They lack adequate resources and defenses. Ava’s defense of the settlement marks her transition into a reliable field partner.

### Hospital and surrounding city

The hospital mission takes place in a ruined urban zone occupied by mutants and scattered survivors. Its archived research becomes the bridge between scavenging and Karrow’s later blood proposal. Harrow steals part of the material; enough remains to continue the work.

## Survivor Society

### Rescue crews

Rescue crews evacuate civilians, recover supplies, maintain cold refuges, and avoid unnecessary mutant kills because dead mutants can release spores. Their work joins humanitarian rescue with contamination control.

### Settlements

Settlements range from organized fortresses to improvised industrial shelters. Scarcity governs food, medicine, power, filters, ammunition, and transport. New arrivals may be treated as labor, risk, or political pressure as much as people.

### Fear of mutation

Fear of Ava is rationally connected to the world’s history but becomes prejudice when people refuse to see her choices or individuality. The novel should preserve both sides: survivors have genuine reasons to fear loss of control, and Ava has genuine reason to fear being caged or used.

### Economy

Rations and power cells as trade standards are supporting worldbuilding but are not yet established in the novel’s scenes. They may be used in future revisions if introduced naturally. They should not be treated as a fully universal currency without an author decision.

## Technology Level

The setting combines rare surviving high technology with widespread scarcity.

- Cryogenic suspension is old-world apex technology and cannot be casually reproduced.
- Filters, gas masks, spore sensors, medical tools, energy cells, firearms, vehicles, and carrier systems remain usable through salvage and maintenance.
- Advanced systems are difficult to replace. A functioning device is more often inherited or repaired than newly manufactured.
- Technology should feel physical, worn, rationed, and dependent on skilled crew.

## Reserved World Material

Large regional lists, named factions, shops, equipment economies, and mission hubs from game or sequel development are not automatically canon to this novel.

The following names are specifically reserved until used or approved: The Northern Coalition, The Crimson Syndicate, The Horizon Initiative, The Frostborn Vanguard, The Sporelands, The White Graveyard, The Lost Ocean, and the wider region taxonomy.
