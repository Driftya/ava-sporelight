# Back Cover

The spores were supposed to change the future. Instead, they ended the world.

Ava was a botanist before an alien infection made her the final subject of a brutal experiment. Sealed in cryogenic sleep as civilization collapsed, she wakes two centuries later with shattered memories, a body she barely recognizes, and blood that can destroy the mutations stalking humanity’s last survivors.

Gabriel is the rescue operative who finds her—and the first person to see more than the weapon she has become. His trust offers Ava a fragile place among his crew, but safety is never simple. Fear follows her aboard the ship. Her power threatens to consume her. And in the ruins below, human betrayal may prove as deadly as the creatures born from the spores.

As Ava and Gabriel fight side by side through fallen fortresses, abandoned settlements, and the shadows of a ruined hospital, their bond grows into something neither terror nor time could erase. Yet the research they recover forces Ava to face the choice she has dreaded since the laboratory: surrender part of herself to science again, or abandon humanity’s best chance to heal.

To reclaim the future, Ava must become more than the experiment that destroyed her past. She must decide for herself whether she is humanity’s cure, its greatest threat—or the first light of a world being born anew.

*Ava: Sporelight* is a post-apocalyptic science-fiction romance about bodily autonomy, survival, betrayal, and the stubborn forms of life that take root after the end.
