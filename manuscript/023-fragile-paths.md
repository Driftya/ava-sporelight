# Chapter 23: Fragile Paths

The ruins of the city stretched out like a maze, each turn a calculated risk as the trio pushed closer to their goal. The hospital loomed nearer, its silhouette jagged and ominous against the dimming sky. The weight of exhaustion was starting to show on Harrow, his steps faltering more frequently, though he pressed on without complaint.

“We need to stop soon,” Gabriel said, glancing at Ava. “The light’s fading, and we can’t risk getting caught out in the open at night.”

Ava surveyed the area, her instincts sharpened by the tension in the air. “There,” she said, pointing to a partially intact office building. “It’s not ideal, but it’ll give us some cover.”

Gabriel nodded, leading the way toward the building. They entered cautiously, stepping over shattered glass and broken furniture. The air inside was stale, but it felt safer than the exposed streets.

“We’ll clear the area and secure it,” Gabriel instructed. “Harrow, stay here.”

Harrow nodded, sinking onto a dust-covered chair. “Not going anywhere,” he muttered.

Ava and Gabriel moved through the building, checking each room with practiced efficiency. The emptiness was both a relief and a source of unease. It was too quiet, the kind of silence that made Ava’s skin crawl.

“Clear,” Gabriel said, stepping out of the last room and meeting Ava in the hallway.

She nodded, though her grip on her blade didn’t relax. “Let’s set up some alarms. We can’t afford any surprises.”

Together, they rigged a simple warning system using cans and string, draping the makeshift alarms across the entrances and stairwells. By the time they finished, the last rays of sunlight had disappeared, leaving the building cloaked in darkness.

The trio sat around a dim lantern in the center of the room they’d chosen as their base for the night. Harrow’s face was drawn, his eyes distant as he stared at the flame. Ava could see the weight of the journey wearing on him, though he remained silent.

“What did your sister work on?” Ava asked gently, breaking the quiet.

Harrow looked up, his expression shadowed. “She was trying to reverse it—the mutation. Said she found something, but she…” He trailed off, his jaw tightening. “I don’t know if she finished. I just… I need to know.”

Gabriel exchanged a glance with Ava before speaking. “We’ll do what we can. But you need to stay focused. If there’s anything left at that lab, we’ll find it.”

As Harrow’s words lingered in the air, Gabriel’s mind turned to the mission briefing Jenna had given them before they left. She’d mentioned rumors of research documents in the hospital—something tied to the mutations. Could Harrow’s sister’s work be the same thing they were sent to find? The thought gnawed at him, but he kept it to himself for now.

Harrow nodded, though his hands fidgeted in his lap. “Thanks. Both of you.”

The night passed uneventfully, the alarms untouched. At dawn, the trio prepared to set out again. Gabriel, who had been awake longer than Ava, finally relented to her insistence that she take the watch that night. Her sharp eyes had traced the outlines of the night, ensuring no threat approached while Gabriel and Harrow rested. The night had stretched long, filled with fleeting moments of uneasy sleep and the ever-present weight of vigilance. Every distant sound—a rustling wind, the echo of something shifting in the ruins—kept them on edge, but nothing came.

As dawn crept over the horizon, bathing the landscape in pale light, their path became clearer. The city’s skeletal remains stood like silent sentinels around them, the remnants of a world long past. In the distance, the hospital finally emerged from the mist, its towering frame half-consumed by vines and decay.

The sight of the hospital looming closer bolstered their spirits. Yet, with every step forward, the air grew heavier, as if the building itself was exhaling a warning. The tension coiled in their chests, unspoken but understood.

“Stay sharp,” Gabriel said, his voice low but firm. “We’re almost there, but that’s when things tend to go wrong.”

Ava nodded, her blade ready in her hand. Harrow walked between them, his eyes flicking nervously to every shadow. The path ahead was fraught with danger, but they pressed on, their resolve unshaken as they approached the threshold of what might be their greatest challenge yet.
